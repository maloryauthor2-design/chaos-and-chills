About Malory

A return to the adventures that used to pull kids into reading.

I started writing these for my own son, more or less, and the only rule I've stuck to is that every chapter needs a reason to keep reading, which mostly means no lectures, no detours and no easy outs, though the odd detour sneaks in when a monster demands it. If a book earns the late-night torch under the duvet, it's done its job.

When I'm not writing them I'm researching a doctorate at the University of Worcester on why children stop reading for pleasure, which turns out to be the same question the books are trying to answer. I also won the 2026 Jim Baen Memorial Award and review short fiction for Tangent Online, and the books come out through Watchful Wizard and Raconteur Press.
